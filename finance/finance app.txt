# 💰 Finance App — Complete System Design

The **Finance App** is the financial management backbone of the Pritech System. It manages quotations, invoices, payments, expenses, taxes, financial reports, and business performance analytics.

---

# 🎯 Purpose

The Finance App enables Pritech to:

* Create quotations
* Generate branded PDF invoices
* Track payments
* Manage expenses
* Monitor cash flow
* Generate financial reports
* Analyze profitability
* Support business decision-making

---

# 👥 Users Involved

## 🏢 Client

Can:

* View quotations
* Accept or reject quotations
* View invoices
* View payment history
* Download PDFs

---

## 💰 Finance Officer

Can:

* Create quotations
* Create invoices
* Record payments
* Manage expenses
* Generate reports
* Export reports

---

## 👨‍💼 Administrator

Can:

* Access all financial records
* Configure taxes
* Configure branding
* View all reports
* Audit transactions

---

## 🧑‍🔧 Technician

Limited involvement:

* Submit work logs
* Trigger billable work
* View assigned service costs (optional)

---

## 🤖 AI Engine

Uses finance data to:

* Forecast revenue
* Predict late payments
* Analyze profitability
* Recommend pricing
