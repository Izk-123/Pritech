# 📊 Tracking App — Complete System Design

The **Tracking App** is the intelligence and monitoring layer of the Pritech System. Its purpose is to capture, monitor, analyze, and provide insights from every meaningful activity occurring within the system and on the public website.

Unlike the other apps, the Tracking App does not directly manage business operations. Instead, it observes and analyzes them.

---

# 🎯 Purpose

The Tracking App enables Pritech to:

* Track user activities
* Track client behavior
* Track website visitors
* Monitor service performance
* Monitor ticket performance
* Monitor financial performance
* Generate business intelligence
* Feed data into the AI Engine
* Detect anomalies and security threats

# 🚨 Important Design Decision

The Tracking App should **not duplicate data** from Finance, Tickets, Services, or Clients.

Instead:

```text
Finance App
      ↓
Provides financial data

Tickets App
      ↓
Provides support data

Services App
      ↓
Provides service data

Tracking App
      ↓
Aggregates and analyzes everything
```

This reduces redundancy and keeps the architecture clean.

---

# 🎯 Main Goal of the Tracking App

> **To collect, monitor, analyze, and visualize operational, financial, customer, and website activity data across the entire Pritech System, providing real-time business intelligence, performance monitoring, security auditing, and the data foundation for AI-driven decision making.** 🚀📊🤖

The Tracking App is essentially the **eyes and ears of the Pritech System**, turning raw system activity into actionable insights for management and future AI capabilities.
