# 👥 Clients App — Complete System Design

The **Clients App** is the customer relationship and client management module of the Pritech System. It serves as the central source of truth for all clients, organizations, contacts, contracts, services, quotations, invoices, tickets, and interactions.

---

# 🎯 Purpose

The Clients App enables Pritech to:

* Register and manage clients
* Store client information
* Track client relationships
* Manage client contacts
* Monitor services purchased
* View client financial status
* View client support history
* Analyze client value and engagement

---

# 🧠 Core Idea

```text
One Client
    ↓
Many Services
    ↓
Many Tickets
    ↓
Many Quotations
    ↓
Many Invoices
```

The Clients App becomes the **Customer 360° View** of the entire ERP.

# 🎯 Main Goal of the Clients App

> **To provide a centralized customer management platform that stores, organizes, and tracks all client information, services, interactions, contracts, tickets, quotations, invoices, and relationships, enabling Pritech to deliver excellent customer service and build long-term client relationships.**

## Position Within Pritech ERP

```text
Portfolio App
      ↓
Potential Client
      ↓
Clients App
      ↓
Services App
      ↓
Tickets App
      ↓
Finance App
      ↓
Analytics & AI
```

This makes the **Clients App the heart of customer relationship management (CRM)** within the Pritech System. 🚀👥📊💼
