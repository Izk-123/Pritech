# 🔐 Accounts App — Complete System Design

The **Accounts App** is the identity, authentication, authorization, and security foundation of the entire Pritech System.

Every user in the system must pass through the Accounts App.

---

# 🎯 Purpose

The Accounts App is responsible for:

* Authentication (Login/Logout)
* Authorization (Permissions & Roles)
* User Management
* Security
* Audit Logging
* Session Management
* User Profiles
* Account Verification
* Real-time Presence Tracking

---

# 🧠 Core Philosophy

For Pritech:

✅ **Clients can self-register**

❌ Internal staff cannot self-register

Staff accounts are created by Administrators.

# 🗄 Database Design

---

## User

Custom user model.

```python
class User(AbstractBaseUser, PermissionsMixin):
    id = models.UUIDField(primary_key=True)

    email = models.EmailField(unique=True, null=True, blank=True)

    phone_number = models.CharField(
        max_length=20,
        unique=True,
        null=True,
        blank=True
    )

    first_name = models.CharField(max_length=100)
    last_name = models.CharField(max_length=100)

    national_id = models.CharField(max_length=50)

    physical_address = models.TextField()

    is_active = models.BooleanField(default=True)

    is_staff = models.BooleanField(default=False)

    is_verified = models.BooleanField(default=False)

    date_joined = models.DateTimeField(auto_now_add=True)
```

---

## Why Email OR Phone?

Many Malawian clients may not use email frequently.

Allow:

```text

Phone only
Email + Phone
```

Login can use either.

# 🎯 Main Goal of the Accounts App

> **To provide secure authentication, flexible role-based authorization, user identity management, session monitoring, audit logging, and real-time account services for all users of the Pritech System while supporting future scalability and enterprise-grade security.**

### Position in the System

```text
Accounts App
      ↓
Clients App
      ↓
Services App
      ↓
Tickets App
      ↓
Finance App
      ↓
Tracking & AI
```

The **Accounts App is the foundation of the entire Pritech ERP**, because every action in the system is performed by an authenticated and authorized user. 🔐🚀
