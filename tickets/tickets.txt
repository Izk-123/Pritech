# 🎫 Tickets App — Complete System Design

The **Tickets App** is the operational heart of the Pritech System. It manages all client support requests, incidents, service requests, maintenance tasks, project issues, and internal technical work.

This module ensures that every client issue is tracked, assigned, monitored, resolved, and analyzed efficiently.

---

# 🎯 Purpose

The Tickets App enables Pritech to:

* Receive client support requests
* Manage incidents and problems
* Assign technicians
* Monitor service delivery
* Enforce Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
* Track work progress
* Improve response times
* Measure technician performance
* Maintain support history

---
